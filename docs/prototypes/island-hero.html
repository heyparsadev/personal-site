About

I run Sibkade, and I’m building Barayand, a multi-model AI platform for Persian speakers. I trained as a psychologist and write about AI in Persian most days.

Disagreement between models is signal.

Barayand sends one question to a panel of frontier models and writes one answer, with the confidence attached.

Restraint builds trust.

My psychology bot’s best feature is what it refuses to do.

Measure it, or you don’t know it.

There was no Persian benchmark, so I built one.
